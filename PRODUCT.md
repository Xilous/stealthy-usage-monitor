# Product

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

Windows users monitoring Claude, Codex and Antigravity quotas while working.
The native desktop widget shows percentage used and reset countdowns at a glance.

## Design principles

- Keep the existing compact native interface and appearance settings.
- Label providers and quota windows; do not rely on color alone.
- Distinguish unavailable readings from zero usage.
- Preserve readable figures and monitor DPI scaling.
- Keep monitoring lightweight and avoid additional network polls for animation.

## Scope

Extend the existing Claude display with reported Fable quota usage and reset time.
Retain the established typography, colors and gauge treatment.
